# Open Margin: indexing readiness

This note keeps the journal's indexing plans accurate. Open Margin is not in Scopus, Web of Science, DOAJ, or another abstracting service. Do not describe it as indexed until an official title search confirms that status.

## Current position

- The journal has a live website and a public Volume 01 record.
- The journal has one published article at the time of this note.
- The site has public policies, a review description, an editor-only submission desk, and a public article page.
- No ISSN has been assigned yet.

## Next publication work

1. Publish at least four more articles that complete the first issue.
2. Keep the issue page, article pages, author names, dates, abstracts, and citation details stable.
3. Record the editorial board, review model, publication frequency, and corrections process in public pages.
4. Keep a private log of submissions, decisions, conflicts, reviewer invitations, and corrections.
5. Apply for an online ISSN through the correct national centre when the first issue is complete and the publication record is ready.
6. Check each service's current title criteria before applying. An ISSN does not guarantee acceptance by an index.

The ISSN International Centre states that online resources are usually not assigned an ISSN before publication and that five articles are a minimum for a complete new issue. See the [ISSN assignment rules](https://www.issn.org/understanding-the-issn/assignment-rules/issn-the-major-principles/) and the [ISSN national centres directory](https://www.issn.org/services/requesting-an-issn/national-centres/).

## Evidence to keep

- A stable journal title and domain
- A clear publisher or responsible organization
- A visible archive of issues and articles
- Consistent article metadata and citation information
- Public ethics, privacy, copyright, correction, and conflict policies
- A functioning contact address
- Evidence that decisions are independent and that authors are not charged for acceptance

## What to say to partners

Use this wording until the status changes:

> Open Margin is a new, free journal for student research. We are building our first complete issue and have not yet applied for an ISSN or indexing. We publish our review process and policies so partners can evaluate the project as it develops.

Do not promise Scopus inclusion, an ISSN, a DOI, admissions value, or a publication date.
