# Open Margin: 30-day growth plan

The problem is not a lack of possible audiences. The problem is that the project does not yet have enough trust evidence. The next month should create one complete review cycle, a small reviewer bench, and one program referral. More broad outreach comes after those pieces work.

## Main goal

Complete one high-quality review cycle and use it to earn one qualified program conversation.

## Audience

Start with research mentorship programs, school research coordinators, AP Research teachers, and nonprofit research organizations. They already work with authors who have finished papers. They can forward an optional resource without sending student data.

Do not target programs that want guaranteed publication, admissions results, priority review, or student data.

## Message

> When a student finishes a research project, Open Margin gives them one optional place to get two written reviews and a decision letter without paying a fee or giving up control of the work.

## Days 1 to 7: make the process credible

- Invite three qualified adult reviewers or advisors.
- Ask two reviewers to complete a short rubric calibration.
- Record the current funnel baseline: visits, guide visits, partner inquiries, reviewer applications, submission starts, completed submissions, and response status.
- Keep the [share page](/share) and [partner page](/partners) consistent with the public [review process](/review).

## Days 8 to 20: run a small test

- Send 20 tailored messages to the correct program contact.
- Use one follow-up after the recipient's stated response window.
- Invite one trusted author to submit a real or clearly labeled test manuscript.
- Process the submission through screening, two reviews, an editorial decision, and author communication.

## Days 21 to 30: decide what to change

Write a short retrospective. Record:

- Where authors hesitated
- What reviewers needed
- Actual time for each step
- Which messages earned a real reply
- Which form or policy confused people

If fewer than two qualified reviewers agree to participate, narrow the fields promised on the site. If 20 tailored messages produce fewer than two substantive replies, revise the audience or message before sending more.

## Outreach rules

- Send small, tailored batches.
- Contact organizations, not student email lists.
- Disclose that you run Open Margin.
- Never promise acceptance, admissions value, or a fixed decision date.
- Follow each community's rules before posting. Ask moderators when approval is required.
- Do not send a second follow-up unless the recipient asks for more information.

## Success measures

The first useful numbers are:

1. Two qualified reviewers and one backup
2. Twenty tailored program contacts
3. Three substantive replies
4. One complete review cycle
5. One program willing to share the optional resource

Do not add paid advertising or charge authors before these measures are tested. The current free author model is the trust wedge.

## Longer-term support

After the first completed cycles, test grants, donations, or bounded sponsor support for reviewer capacity. Payment must never buy acceptance, priority, faster review, or access to student data.
