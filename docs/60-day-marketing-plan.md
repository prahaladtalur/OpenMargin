# Open Margin: 60-day operating plan

This plan runs from August 31 through October 30, 2026. It turns outreach into
evidence while protecting the review timeline. It does not promise a number of
submissions or publications.

## Starting point

- One article is public in Volume 01.
- Four targeted partner emails were sent on August 31 to Indigo Research,
  Young Minds Research Foundation, Lumiere Education, and RISE Global
  Education.
- Those four messages are labeled `Open Margin/Partner Outreach` in Gmail.
- The next capacity milestone is two qualified reviewers and one backup.

| Oct 20 to 30 | Compare results and set the next safe capacity. Keep only channels that produce good-fit work or useful feedback. | Completed and eligible submissions, reviewer applications, partner replies, decisions, and published articles. |

## Outreach rules

- Send no more than five new organizational messages in one week.
- Send one follow-up only after at least five business days, unless the
  recipient asks for more information.
- Use public organizational addresses. Do not buy lists or contact students
  directly without permission.
- Use a tagged `/guide`, `/share`, or `/submit` link for each channel.
- State that Open Margin is free, independent, early-stage, and not indexed in
  Scopus. Do not promise acceptance, admissions value, priority, or a fixed
  decision date.
- Stop a channel when it produces repeated off-scope work, pressure on minors,
  or requests for special treatment.

## Capacity gate

Do not widen the fields shown on the site or send a larger campaign until two
reviewers and one backup can cover the current scope. Do not apply for an ISSN
or claim indexing during this period. Build the public record first.
