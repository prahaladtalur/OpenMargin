# Open Margin reviewer recruitment pack

Use this pack to recruit one more qualified reviewer and one backup. Send no
more than three new reviewer messages in one week. Use a public work address or
an existing professional contact. Do not send a manuscript or a student name.

## Adult reviewer or advisor

**Subject:** Would you consider advising a new student journal?

Hello [name],

I run Open Margin, a small, free journal for student research in the
humanities, social sciences, and STEM. We are building a careful reviewer
bench before we widen outreach.

Would you be willing to read our public rubric and tell me if the safeguards
are clear? If your field and availability fit a future manuscript, you could
also choose to review one blinded paper. There is no obligation to accept an
assignment. We ask every reviewer to disclose conflicts and keep manuscripts
confidential.

Process and application: https://openmargin.org/reviewers
Review rubric: https://openmargin.org/review

Thank you for considering it,

Prahalad Talur
Open Margin

## Research methods or library specialist

**Subject:** A small request about research review

Hello [name],

I am building Open Margin, a free journal for student research. I am looking
for a small number of adults who can help with research methods, citations,
source access, or research communication when a paper needs that expertise.

Could you review the public process and tell me what is unclear? If you later
choose to apply, we match work to your field, capacity, and conflicts. The
editorial team makes the decision.

Public process: https://openmargin.org/review
Application: https://openmargin.org/reviewers

Thank you for your time,

Prahalad Talur

## Permission-based program share

**Subject:** Optional reviewer pathway for student research

Hello [name],

Open Margin is a free, student-led journal for research by authors of any age.
We are currently building reviewer capacity, not asking programs to send
student data or manuscripts.

If you know an adult researcher, teacher, librarian, or research adviser who
may want to review the public process, please share this link only if it fits
your normal communication rules: https://openmargin.org/reviewers

Thank you for considering the optional resource,

Prahalad Talur

## Follow-up rule

Send one follow-up after five business days. If there is no reply, close the
thread. Record the date, field, and outcome in the private log. Do not chase a
reviewer who has declined or asked not to be contacted.
